The Romantic Setup

A slow, shared moment — not rushed, not one-sided

The Couples Foot Ritual was designed for two people to be pampered at the same time, in the same room, without either of you waiting your turn. It's become one of our most requested bookings for anniversaries, date nights and thoughtful gifts in Rustenburg.

Two Copper Foot Baths

Warm water infused with rose petals, rosemary and eucalyptus, set up side by side so you soak together from the very first minute.

Side-by-Side Reflexology

Full sole, arch and toe reflexology performed on both of you in the same session — roughly 25-30 minutes of dedicated attention each.

Aromatic Oils for Two

Lavender, chamomile and eucalyptus blends applied to finish, chosen to relax rather than overwhelm — no synthetic fragrance.

Approx. 60 Minutes Total

Booked as one combined appointment for both guests. Extend with an additional Hot Stone Foot Therapy add-on on request.
