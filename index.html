Common questions

Before you book

The things clients ask most often — anything else, just message Siyabonga.

Do you come to my home or office?

Yes. Sole Sacraty is a mobile foot spa: copper foot baths, heated stones, towels and oils travel with us to your home or office anywhere in Gauteng. You can also visit the Rustenburg studio.

How much does a session cost?

Foot reflexology is R350 for roughly 50 minutes, hot stone foot therapy is R450 for roughly 65 minutes, and the couples foot ritual is R600 for two guests for roughly 60 minutes.

Is reflexology the same as a foot massage?

No. Reflexology applies targeted pressure to specific points mapped across the sole, arch and toes, rather than working the muscles for general relaxation. We do strictly foot therapy — no full-body massage.

Do I need to pay a deposit?

Mobile sessions in Gauteng are confirmed with a 50% deposit. Rustenburg studio visits are paid on arrival.

How do I pay, and how do I know it is safe?

You can pay by card through a secure Yoco link, by SnapScan, or by bank transfer using details we send you privately on WhatsApp. We never publish banking details online and never ask for payment into a personal account. If in doubt, phone +27 70 493 8532 to verify before paying.

Which areas do you serve?

The Rustenburg studio serves Rustenburg and the wider North West, and mobile sessions cover Gauteng, including Johannesburg, Pretoria, Centurion, the East Rand and Springs.
