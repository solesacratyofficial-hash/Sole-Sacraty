The Romantic Setup

A slow, shared moment — not rushed, not one-sided

The Couples Foot Ritual was designed for two people to be pampered at the same time, in the same room, without either of you waiting your turn. It's become one of our most requested bookings because it combines relaxation with genuine shared presence — something rare in a world of back-to-back appointments.

Two Copper Foot Baths

Warm water infused with rose petals, rosemary and eucalyptus, set up side by side so you soak together from the very first minute. Each bath is heated and kept at a comfortable temperature throughout your session.

Side-by-Side Reflexology

Full sole, arch and toe reflexology performed on both of you in the same session — roughly 25-30 minutes of dedicated attention each. Siyabonga works on one guest's feet while you both experience the same therapeutic techniques simultaneously.

Aromatic Oils for Two

Lavender, chamomile and eucalyptus blends applied to finish, chosen to relax rather than overwhelm — no synthetic fragrance. The ritual ends with a grounding oil massage to leave both guests feeling anchored and at ease.

Approx. 60 Minutes Total

Booked as one combined appointment for both guests. Extend with an additional Hot Stone Foot Therapy add-on on request (adds 15 minutes, +R150 per guest). Perfect timing for lunch dates or evening unwind together.

Gift Voucher Option
